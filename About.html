About the Department

The Department of Information Technology at ISBAT University is committed to producing skilled, innovative and ethical IT professionals who can solve real problems in Uganda and beyond.

Our teaching combines theory with practical laboratory work, covering software development, networking, databases, cybersecurity and web technologies.

Our Mission

To provide quality, practical IT education that prepares graduates for employment, entrepreneurship and lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of excellence in information technology education in the region.
