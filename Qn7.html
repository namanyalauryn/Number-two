Supplier Profile

Chromelite Technologies Limited is a Kampala-based supplier of branding, signage and structural steel. From our base in Naalya, we serve businesses, event organisers and institutions that need dependable branded products delivered on time.

This page introduces what we supply and why customers choose us. Our current price schedule is on the Pricing page.

Why Choose Chromelite?

Branded Stationery

We print notebooks, business cards and pens that carry your brand consistently. Every batch is checked for colour accuracy before delivery.

Event Materials

Roll-up banners, vinyl banners and lanyards are produced on tough outdoor-grade media. We deliver to your venue ahead of the event date.

Branded Apparel

Polo shirts, T-shirts and caps are decorated with durable prints and embroidery. Sizes and quantities can be adjusted to suit your team.
